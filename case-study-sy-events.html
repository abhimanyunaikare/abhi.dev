Project Overview

Sahaja Yoga Events Platform

A purpose-built ticketing and coupon management system for Sahaja Yoga — a spiritual organisation holding monthly programmes, pujas and workshops across India with hundreds to thousands of attendees.

The Brief
"We need members to register digitally, pay online, receive a QR coupon, and be checked in at the gate — with organisers able to manage everything from their phones."
Client
Sahaja Yoga Organisation, Pune
Timeline
3 months · Ongoing
My Role
Solo full-stack developer — architecture, design, build, deployment
Platform
Web (mobile-first, works on any device with no app install)
The Problem

Registration was running on goodwill and group chats

Before this platform, every event was a coordination nightmare handled entirely through WhatsApp groups, paper registers, and manual payment collection at the gate.

📱
WhatsApp flooding

Members sent names and payment screenshots to group chats. Coordinators manually tracked each one in Excel sheets, prone to duplicates and missed entries.

💸
Cash-only payments with no records

No digital payment trail, no receipt, no way for accounts team to reconcile collections after the event. Donations were especially hard to track.

🚪
Gate chaos on event day

Check-in volunteers had printed name lists. Searching for a name under crowd pressure, marking it off with a pen, handling last-minute registrations — every event was stressful.

📊
Zero post-event data

No visibility into attendance by age, gender, or city. No way to know who volunteered, what people's professions were, or how many first-time seekers attended.

How It Was Built

Eight modules, built in sequence

Each phase delivered working, usable functionality — not just code. The client could test and give feedback at every stage.

🏗️
Phase 1
Foundation & Database Design
Designed a PostgreSQL schema covering events, ticket tiers, members, families, volunteers, donations, OTP codes, and zone options. Built role-based JWT authentication for five distinct user types. Deployed on Supabase with pooler connections for reliability.
PostgreSQLSupabaseJWT AuthNode.js
🎟️
Phase 2
Coupon Booking & Payment
Built the complete booking flow: age-based automatic pricing (Child / Yuva / Adult), sex-based differential rates, discount codes, Razorpay UPI/card/wallet integration, and instant QR coupon generation. Free children get no QR — just a confirmed entry. Donation step built in before payment.
RazorpayQR GenerationDynamic PricingDiscount Codes
📲
Phase 3
OTP Login & Member Accounts
Members log in via mobile OTP using MSG91. First-time users set up a profile capturing name, sex, date of birth (auto-calculates age category), city/zone, profession, and seva interests. Family members can be added and saved for one-click booking at future events.
MSG91 OTPFamily ProfilesDOB auto-ageProfession field
📷
Phase 4
Mobile Check-in Scanner
Check-in seva volunteers open a web page on their phone — no app install needed. Camera opens, QR is scanned, result shows green (admitted) or red (already checked in). Manual search by name, phone, or email with a one-tap check-in button for when QR can't be scanned.
HTML5 CameraReal-timeOne-time entryManual fallback
📊
Phase 5
Organiser Dashboard & Accounts
Organisers see live stats per event — registered, checked in, remaining. Accounts team gets a separate login with full financial breakdown: ticket revenue, donations, discount impact, Razorpay transaction IDs, year-wise comparison, and grouped booking view for multi-member payments.
Role-based viewsCSV exportPDF printYear comparison
🙌
Phase 6
Volunteer & Seva Directory
Every member and family member can declare seva interests (photography, gardening, web development, prasad distribution, etc.) and profession. Admin sees a searchable directory filterable by interest, profession, age category, and city — with CSV export for planning seva teams.
Volunteer profilesMulti-filterAdmin-only
⚙️
Phase 7
Admin Settings & Configuration
Organisers manage event zones/cities and seva interest options through a settings panel — add, edit, hide, or delete without touching code. Zone picker on registration forms is a searchable autocomplete populated from this list.
Dynamic configNo-code adminSearchable zones
🚀
Phase 8
Deployment & Cost Optimisation
Frontend on Vercel (free), backend on Render (free tier), database on Supabase (free). Total monthly infrastructure cost: ₹0. A keep-alive cron job prevents Render cold starts. Custom domain support configured. Ready to migrate to VPS when scale demands it.

What Was Built

A complete platform, not just a booking form

Every feature was shaped by how Sahaja Yoga events actually run — not by generic ticketing platform templates.

🪷
Spiritually-aware UX
Language uses "programme" not "event", "member" not "user", "coupon" not "ticket", "dana" alongside donation. Auspicious preset amounts (₹51, ₹101, ₹251, ₹501).
👨‍👩‍👧
Family-first booking
Save family members once, tick their names at every future event. DOB-based auto-categorisation means age tiers update automatically each year without any data entry.
💰
Flexible pricing engine
Per-event pricing matrix: Child/Yuva/Adult × Male/Female. Sex-based differential is optional per event. Members can override their suggested tier (e.g. non-working adult choosing Yuva). Free children get no QR — just confirmed entry.
🔐
Five user roles
Admin, Organiser, Check-in Seva, Registration Seva, Accounts. Every role sees only what it needs — accounts never sees check-in, check-in never sees financials.
📱
No app install needed
Members book on any browser. Check-in volunteers scan QR from their phone camera through the web. No Play Store, no App Store, no updates to manage.
🧾
Accounts-ready reporting
Razorpay transaction IDs per coupon, grouped bookings view for multi-member single payments, year-wise comparison, donation tracking separate from ticket revenue.
📧
Delivery on multiple channels
QR coupon delivered via email and WhatsApp simultaneously. Downloadable image includes name, age category, venue, booking date, and phone number — ready to screenshot and share.
🌐
9 Indian languages ready
Full translation support for Hindi, Marathi, Gujarati, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Bengali, and Punjabi — switchable per seeker, stored in their browser preference.
👤
Guest booking supported
Members can book and pay without creating an account. Their coupons are linked to their phone number and can be retrieved any time by logging in later.

Access Design

Five roles, each with exactly the access it needs

Role-based access was one of the most thoughtfully designed parts of the system — every role sees a completely different interface.

👑 Super Admin
  • All organiser capabilities
  • Volunteer directory (profession + seva)
  • User management & password reset
  • Settings: zones & seva options
  • Full accounts access
🗂️ Organiser
  • Create, edit, publish programmes
  • Event dashboard & attendee list
  • Discount code management
  • CSV export
  • Check-in scanner access
📷 Check-in Seva
  • View active programmes only
  • QR scanner on mobile browser
  • Manual lookup by name / phone / email
  • Live check-in count
  • No financial data visible
📊 Accounts Team
  • Revenue per programme
  • Donation tracking
  • Razorpay transaction IDs
  • Grouped booking view
  • Year-wise comparison & export
